## Press play. That's the whole workflow.

Auto-Subs is a Stremio addon. You press play, it finds the original English subtitle for that movie or episode, translates it into the language you actually understand, and hands it back to Stremio in about a minute. Every cue keeps its original timing. Nothing to edit, nothing to upload, nothing to install on your TV.

Once a title has been translated for one viewer, every viewer after that gets it instantly.

> **Try it in 30 seconds:** install the addon, play *Breaking Bad* S01E01, open Stremio's subtitles menu and pick Auto-Subs. That episode is already translated into every supported language, so your subtitles appear immediately.

## How it works

1. **Install once.** One link into Stremio from the [install guide](https://auto-subs.com/install.html). It works everywhere your Stremio account is signed in.
2. **Pick your language.** Set the language you actually understand in the addon's settings. Change it whenever you want.
3. **Play.** Auto-Subs finds the original subtitle, translates it in the background, and it's ready in about a minute. Open Stremio's subtitles menu and choose Auto-Subs.

Behind the scenes: two paid subtitle providers plus two community sources are searched for the best English file for your exact release. If no English subtitle exists yet (a brand-new episode, a Chinese or Turkish production), Auto-Subs translates from the best file in any other language it can find, and keeps checking for seven days so your next play is served from cache without you lifting a finger.

## Giving back: free subtitle tools (open source)

We spend our days inside subtitle files, and a lot of what we learn is useful far beyond Auto-Subs. This is our way of giving something back to the subtitle and translation community: small, open-source tools that run entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded — and help whether or not you ever install the addon.

- **[Subtitle Toolbox](subtitles-tools/subtitle-toolbox/)** — fix garbled Hebrew, Arabic, Cyrillic or Greek encoding, shift or retime subtitles, convert SRT/VTT/ASS, and check reading speed and overlaps.
