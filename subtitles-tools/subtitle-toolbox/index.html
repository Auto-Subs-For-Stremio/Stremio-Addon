Fix, retime, convert and check subtitle files

Drop an SRT, VTT or ASS file. Garbled Hebrew, Arabic, Cyrillic or Greek text gets its encoding repaired, timing can be shifted or stretched to match your video, formats convert both ways, and a quick check finds overlaps and lines that are too fast to read. Every tool works offline on your own machine.

Fix encoding

Subtitles that show as boxes, question marks or "×©×œ×•×" were saved in a legacy code page or decoded twice. Pick the reading that looks right; the download is clean UTF-8, which every player understands.

Shift or retime

Subtitles early or late by a fixed amount? Shift them. Drifting more and more as the film goes on? That is a different cut or frame rate: give two anchor points, or pick the frame rates, and everything in between is stretched to match.

Convert

SRT is what most players and Stremio want. ASS files from anime releases convert to plain SRT (styling and positioning are dropped, text and timing kept). VTT is for web players.

Hebrew / Arabic

Two classic right-to-left problems. Punctuation at the wrong end of the line ("?מה קורה") is fixed by pinning each RTL line with direction marks that players honor. Text with every word spelled backwards comes from old tools that stored visual order; it can be flipped back.

Check quality

Overlapping cues, cues too short to read, reading speed above 20 characters per second, lines over 42 characters, more than two lines, empty cues, and leftover encoding garbage.
